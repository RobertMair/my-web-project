CANBERRA MODERN

Showcasing Canberra’s unique mid and late twentieth century design heritage, Canberra Modern’s motto is Conservation Through Participation.

Our aim is to increase awareness of Canberra’s modernist character, heritage and uniqueness via innovative events and community engagement.

Architecture

Canberra grew up in the mid-century period and showcases work from some of Australia’s most renowned architects.

Events

Through event-based advocacy and community engagement, Canberra Modern aims to promote protection and appreciation of the places which make an irreplaceable contribution to Canberra’s historic urban and designed cultural landscape.
